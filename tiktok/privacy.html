Privacy Policy — Channel Stats (internal tool)

Last updated: 2026-10-09

This app is a private, internal analytics tool operated by the owner of the TikTok accounts it connects to. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

With the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app reads: basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (follower count, likes count, video count) and the list of the owner's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How we use it

The data is used only to produce a daily performance report for the account owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and daily statistics are stored on the owner's private server. Statistics are kept for trend comparison and can be deleted at any time on request. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
